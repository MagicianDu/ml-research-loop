# Code Of Conduct

This project expects respectful, technical collaboration.

## Standards

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about risks and evidence.
- Discuss tradeoffs and decisions in public project artifacts when possible.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that disrupt
project work or violate these standards. Repeated or severe violations may lead
to temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

## Reporting

Use the repository issue tracker or maintainer contact channel to report
conduct concerns. Keep reports factual and include links or timestamps when
available.
